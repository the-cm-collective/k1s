Do not treat later milestones as active until their listed dependencies have a clear exit decision.

## Update Rules

- Update `Status` when a milestone or phase becomes active, blocked, or complete.
- Keep `Evidence` tied to concrete docs, code, tests, or run artifacts.
- Do not mark a later row `In progress` unless every dependency named in `Depends On` has a clear exit decision or a documented overlap.
- Keep claims conservative. This page is for implementation truth, not aspiration.
